The Problem

Truth in
the noise.

In an era of viral misinformation, distinguishing credible journalism from fabricated content has become one of the most pressing challenges of the internet age. Credibility Compass addresses this directly — using NLP classification to analyse article content and flag likely fake news.

The application processes raw article text, extracting linguistic patterns, structural features, and semantic signals that distinguish credible journalism from fabricated content. The result is a fast, accessible tool anyone can use to verify what they're reading.

How it Works

Features

Article Analysis

Paste any article URL or raw text. The system extracts linguistic and structural features for classification within seconds.

NLP Classification

A trained text classification model analyses writing style, source patterns, and semantic consistency to produce a credibility verdict.

Confidence Scoring

Every verdict comes with a confidence score — so borderline cases are clearly signalled rather than forcing a binary output.

Scale

Built to handle high-volume analysis — suitable for newsrooms, researchers, and fact-checkers who need fast, automated screening.
