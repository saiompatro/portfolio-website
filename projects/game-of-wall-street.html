Overview

Finance
as a game.

Game of Wall Street turns financial mechanics into an engaging Discord game. Players choose from 30+ unique characters — each with distinct stats, backgrounds, and job bonuses — and build wealth through the job system, stock market, and casino.

Every system is interconnected: your character's intellect affects job payouts, your level unlocks higher-tier jobs, and your stock portfolio compounds over time. It's designed to be both entertaining and subtly educational about real economic concepts.

Game Systems

How to play

Character System

Choose from 30+ characters — JP Morgan broker, Stanford grad, self-made CEO, night-shift janitor. Each has unique stats (intellect, wealth, leadership) and job bonuses that shape your entire playthrough.

Job Progression

20 jobs across 4 tiers: Street Vendor → Retail Manager → Lawyer → CEO. Jobs unlock at level milestones and reward players whose character stats align with the role's demands.

Stock Market

20 stocks across tech, finance, semiconductor, crypto, and entertainment sectors with realistic volatility-based price movements. Buy, sell, and build a portfolio for long-term wealth.

Casino

Five casino games — Coinflip, Slots, Blackjack, Roulette, and Dice Roll — each with distinct house edges, bet limits, and risk profiles. Tilt protection prevents runaway losses.

Leveling & Achievements

XP earned from jobs, trading, and casino wins unlocks new tiers. 60+ achievements reward milestones — from first stock purchase to reaching the CEO tier.

Social Economy

Guilds, player duels, and peer-to-peer trading create an emergent social layer. Leaderboards rank players by net worth, level, casino winnings, and job earnings.
