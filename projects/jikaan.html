The Vision

Skills
valued by
the market.

Jikaan is built on a simple but powerful premise: access to knowledge shouldn't be gatekept by cost. The platform enables anyone to teach what they know and learn what they need — with skills valued through genuine supply-and-demand economics.

At its core is the TimeToken system — a proprietary currency earned by teaching and spent on learning. TimeTokens are convertible to real currency, creating a sustainable economy around education and skill-sharing.

The name "Jikaan" (時間) means time in Japanese — a nod to the platform's belief that time spent learning or teaching is the most valuable investment anyone can make.

"Learn for the love of learning, teach for the love of teaching."
Jikaan founding principle

Core Features

How it works

TimeTokens

Earn tokens by teaching skills. Spend them to learn. Tokens reflect real supply and demand — rare skills command higher rates, common ones stay accessible.

Skill Exchange

Peer-to-peer structured sessions between learners and teachers. Sessions are matched by skill category, availability, and proficiency level.

Market Economy

Skill pricing is dynamic — driven by the number of available teachers versus demand. No fixed costs, no gatekeeping.

Real Convertibility

TimeTokens can be converted to real currency, giving educators a tangible return on their knowledge — monetising expertise without barriers.

Community

A growing ecosystem of lifelong learners — students, professionals, and enthusiasts sharing knowledge across disciplines.

Structured Learning

Sessions are structured, not ad-hoc. Jikaan provides frameworks for curriculum design so teachers can deliver consistent, high-quality experiences.
