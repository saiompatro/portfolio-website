About

I'm a builder who likes turning an ambiguous idea into something clear, useful, and ready to meet the world. Right now I'm an AI intern at Fintoo, a SEBI-registered financial advisory, where I ship LLM-based recommendation features for retirement and financial planning into a production mobile app on the App Store and Play Store.

Before that I worked directly with the co-founder at SDAI Synergy Digital on a renewal payment propensity model, and published independent research in IJFMR testing whether rising UPI adoption pushes Indian households into shares and debentures. (A difference-in-differences model across 232 districts said: it doesn't.)

Outside of work I write open-source Claude skills, train sports prediction models, and build tools I wish existed. In the fall I start a B.S. in Computer Science with a Data Science minor at the University of Washington.
