I'm an 18-year-old IBDP student and incoming Computational Finance student at the University of Washington, Seattle — interested in quantitative finance, fintech, and financial data analysis.

My work sits at the intersection of data, algorithms, and real-world financial systems. I interned at Credence Analytics building a sentiment-based stock prediction model, and at SDAI Synergy Digital co-developing an AI insurance renewal predictor.

I've published research on the effect of UPI on Indian household savings (IFJMR) using difference-in-differences regression, and my Extended Essay investigates whether LSTM models outperform regression in predicting Indian stock prices.
